## WHAT THE NIGHT ESTABLISHED (one page)

1. **The programme's goal is continual learning**, and the criterion is
   three numbers: ACQUIRE, RETAIN, NO REGRESSION. The instrument is
   `tools/cl_ruler.py`; the trainer is `tools/cl2_train.py`.
2. **The CL-2 arm trains** (after the detached-leaf / `requires_grad`
   fixes, §16) and its verdict is **ACQUIRE PASS / RETAIN FAIL /
   NO REGRESSION FAIL** (§20): the model's own head recalls the stream's
   facts at **0.8906 exact-4** against a 0.0020 null, retains the
   pre-stream facts at a ratio of **0.0022** against a threshold of 0.90,
   and the held-out text CE goes **3.0695 -> 5.2037 nats/B** — within
   0.34 nats of the unigram baseline.
3. **The state carries the ADDRESS; the weights carry the CONTENT** (§22).
   A fresh ridge readout on the state, split over **board facts** rather
   than rows, scores **exact-4 = 0.0000 in every cell** on both boards.
   The ruler's apparent generalisation on never-trained facts was a
   **table over the board**.
4. **The cause is the TRAINING SIGNAL, not the architecture** (§23). With
   the head's table removed (`--head-frozen`), the memory loss **does not
   fall at all** in 19 steps (armA's falls 4.5 nats in 25). *Caveat: 19
   steps is a strong signal, not a proof.*
5. **P3 is closed as a lever** (§23, M1/M6): the gate **engages** (it
   reaches 0.110 max) but its **between-sample spread is 2.4e-05 of its
   within-sample spread** — it is a global per-dimension re-scaling, not
   selective. And a selective *state* write could not have fixed the
   failure anyway, because the failure was a **table being overwritten**.
6. **The method the night produced**: a measurement's meaning is set by
   its **controls**, and a missing control silently becomes the
   interpretation. Four times tonight: a missing copy control (§17), a
   missing nuisance control (§19), a missing criterion wiring (§20), and a
   **split that let a readout memorise the board** (§22). When a readout is
   fitted, **split over the things it could memorise**.
