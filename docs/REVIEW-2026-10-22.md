# REVIEW — 2026-10-22: what the night produced, and what it means for the programme

**Scope.** A full audit of the night's work (`RESULTS.md` §1–§23), a
section-by-section revision of the master plan
(`docs/plans/PLAN-2026-10-21-CONTINUAL-LEARNING.md`), and a judgment on
whether the programme should continue and on what it should do next.

**Bottom line.** The programme has, for the first time, (a) an instrument
that measures the right thing with the right controls, (b) a **trained**
continual-learning arm with a **complete verdict**, and (c) a **causal
account** of why that verdict is what it is. The goal is **not met**. The
night's real product is not the verdict but the **method** that produced
it — and the most uncomfortable finding of the review is that the night
produced more progress by *correcting the instrument* than by advancing
the goal. That is a warning, and §D says what to do about it.

---

## A. The body of work, judged

### A1. What is SOLID

**The criterion and its instrument.** The three numbers (ACQUIRE, RETAIN,
NO REGRESSION), pre-registered, with a matched null, a nuisance control and
a coverage figure per cell, and an explicit rule that UNMEASURED is never
PASS. This is the single most valuable thing the programme owns. It is
what makes the rest of the night's work interpretable, and it is what made
the §14 misdiagnosis impossible to repeat.

**The result.** `RESULTS.md` §20: a trained arm, scored on the ruler, in
the arm's exact regime, with every control present.

| | measured | threshold | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| ACQUIRE | 0.8906 exact-4 on the stream's facts (null 0.0020) | ≥ 0.50 | **PASS** |
| RETAIN | ratio 0.0022 | ≥ 0.90 | **FAIL** |
| NO REGRESSION | 3.0695 → 5.2037 nats/B (unigram baseline 5.5452) | ≤ +0.02 | **FAIL** |

This is a publishable-quality negative result with a *causal* companion
(§17–§23), and it is the first end-to-end verdict in the programme's
history — 14 plans produced 13 measurable verdicts on proxies and 0 on the
goal.

**The four audits and the method they produced.** §17 (the copy control),
§19 (D5's own copy control), §20 (the criterion's wiring), §22 (the
split). Each caught a wrong conclusion, and each was a case of the same
failure: **a control that was absent silently became the interpretation.**
That is now §9's controls rule, and it is the most transferable thing the
night produced.

**The engineering.** 35 gates, each new one proven to fail on the
pre-change code. The fused AdamW (bit-identical, 1.09× on the whole step).
The `nvrtc` default fix — a 1.26× speedup that had been dead code since
2026-09-24 and was never realised by any run. The dense-credit
reassociation (12× on the contraction, 6.8× end to end) with the
dense-vs-ring bit-identity pin honestly restated as a measured float32
floor rather than quietly loosened. The community CLI, the fork README,
the HF release plan, the upstream note. The history was rewritten from
135.80 MiB to 12.06 MiB with every commit, author, date and message
intact.

### A2. What is SOFT

**The H1 probe is a strong signal, not a proof.** With the head's table
removed the memory loss showed **no downward trend in 19 steps** where the
trainable-head arm fell 4.5 nats in 25 (§23). But 19 steps is short, and
the whole programme's next direction rests on it. **CL-6a (500 steps,
~45 min) exists for exactly this reason and must not be skipped.**

**"D5 proves the state is capable" is an inference, not a measurement of
the CL-2 model.** D5's copy control passes on *D5's* delayed-copy model,
so that model's state genuinely holds a payload. The CL-2 arm is a
different model with a different objective, and the claim "the state is
untrained rather than incapable" is a strong inference from (i) D5's
capability, (ii) H1's failure with the table removed, and (iii) the fact
that the objective never asked. **CL-6b tests it directly.**

**The capacity budget is a model with stated assumptions** — ~1 dim per
address, `log2(alphabet)` dims per byte, one linear readout. The
assumptions are written down and the arithmetic is gated
(`tests/check_state_capacity.py`), so the *arithmetic* is safe; but the
*model* could be wrong in a way that matters (e.g. if the model could use
a non-linear addressing scheme, the budget does not apply). The budget is
used to **order** the arms, not to decide the programme.

**The ACQUIRE number is regime-dependent in a way that is not yet
settled.** 0.8906 exact-4 was measured on the stream's facts (board B) on
fresh streams. The head's advantage over the trained-arm's own training
sequences is not separately characterised, and the ruler's headline
wiring only became correct after the fact.

### A3. What was WRONG, and is now corrected

Five wrong conclusions, four of them the programme's own and one of them
mine, all corrected in the append-only log with the reasoning intact:

| | the wrong claim | the correction |
|---|---|---|
| §14 | the CL-2 arm was "a NULL for a budget reason" | it **never trained** — three silent gradient no-ops (§16) |
| §17, §20, §21 | "the state holds a copy of the payload" | the state holds the **address**; the weights hold the **content** (§22) |
| §20 | ACQUIRE **FAIL** | the ruler's headline was wired to board C; the criterion asks about board B (§20) |
| §22 | the ridge readout "generalises to never-trained facts" | it **memorised a table over the board**; the row split let it (§22) |
| plan §4 | "the lever is the write's selectivity (P3 gate)" | the gate is **not selective** (2.4e-05) and a selective state write could not have fixed a **table** being overwritten (§23) |

**Every one of the five was an instrument error, not a hypothesis error.**
That pattern is the review's most important structural finding.

## C. The programme, judged

**Where it stands.** A recurrent model at this scale, trained on a fact
stream with no replay, **learns the stream's facts and forgets everything
it knew before, while very nearly ceasing to be a language model** — and
the programme knows why, at the level of a causal mechanism and not a
hand-wave.

**What the night did NOT do.** It did not move the goal. It produced a
verdict, a diagnosis, and a method. The distance between "the model
forgets" and "the model does not forget" is exactly the distance the next
three arms have to close, and none of them has been run to completion.

**The uncomfortable finding.** The night's largest single improvement was
finding that the ruler's ridge readout was measuring a **table** instead of
a mechanism — a discovery about the *instrument*, not the *model*. Read
backwards, that is the programme's pattern across 14 plans and one night:
**it is better at auditing itself than at building.** That is a real
danger, because an audit of an instrument always yields a result, while a
successful arm is much harder. **The review's recommendation follows
directly: the next session must spend its time on CL-6a/CL-6b/CL-6c, not
on more audits** — the audits have done their job, and the remaining
uncertainties are all *measurement* uncertainties that only a run can
close.

**What would make me close the programme.** CL-6b (an explicit D5-style
copy circuit) fails **and** CL-6c (the keyed store) fails. Then the
honest conclusion is that a recurrent model at this scale, with these
objectives, does not do continual learning — and the programme becomes an
upstream contribution plus a scale-and-data effort, which is where the
evidence says the remaining headroom is. **That is not a defeat; it is a
result, and it is what §8's kill criterion now says in writing.**

**What would make me continue.** CL-6b succeeds. The state learns to carry
the payload once the objective asks it to, and the programme has a memory —
in which case the work is not hard, it is *scoping*, and the keyed store's
capacity and the replay baseline become the engineering.

## D. What the next session must do, in order

1. **CL-6a — the 500-step H1** (~45 min at batch 32). Make tonight's
   negative conclusive. **Do not skip it**, and do not let a positive
   result be over-read: the loss falling slowly is not the same as the
   state carrying the payload (the copy control is what decides that).
2. **CL-6b — the explicit D5-style copy circuit** (~2 h). The
   training-signal arm. **This is the arm that decides between "the state
   cannot" and "the state was not asked."**
3. **CL-6c — the keyed store (M2)** (~1 day). The architecture the goal
   needs: written once at a key, read many times, no eviction. **Size it
   against the capacity budget, deliberately** — §3's 512 dims was a
   coincidence and the store must not repeat it.
4. **Cheap and outstanding:** the text-only counterfactual (A5) for
   NO REGRESSION's magnitude; the `--memory-alpha 0` control (stopped at
   f2 step 100, **resumable** from `~/tmt-work/runs/cl2/armB_f2_t000100.npz`);
   the **replay baseline**, which the programme still lacks and without
   which "continual learning is hard" is a claim without a reference point.
5. **Then, and not before:** the community deliverables. The fork, the HF
   release and the upstream note all describe a project with a working
   instrument and a documented, unresolved failure — **that is worth
   sending**, and it is worth sending *now* rather than after the result
   is prettier, because the honest version is the interesting one.

**One rule for that session, from tonight:** *a measurement's meaning is
set by its controls.* Every cell carries the null, the nuisance, the copy
control, and — for anything fitted — a split over the things it could
memorise. And **the verdict names its readout.**
